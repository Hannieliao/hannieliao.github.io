**Emilia-NV: A Non-Verbal Speech Dataset with Word-Level Annotation for Human-Like Speech Modeling** \\
**Huan Liao**<sup>★</sup>, Qinke Ni<sup>★</sup>, Yuancheng Wang, Yiheng Lu, Haoyue Zhan, Pengyuan Xie, Qiang Zhang, Zhizheng Wu<sup>†</sup>

[**[Project]**](https://nvspeech170k.github.io/) <strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>
[**[Paper]**](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.04195) <strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>
[**[Dataset]**](https://huggingface.co/datasets/amphion/Emilia-NV) <strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>
[**[Code]**](https://github.com/AmphionTeam//Emilia-NV) <strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>

- The first large-scale dataset with word-level paralinguistic vocalization annotations(573 Hours; 174,179 utterances).
- NVASR, a paralinguistic-aware ASR framework, scales fine-grained automatic annotations.
- Enabled controllable zero-shot TTS(CV2@Emilia-NV) with explicit token-level vocalization control for expressive speech synthesis.

**SpeechJudge: Towards Human-Level Judgment for Speech Naturalness** \\
Xueyao Zhang, Chaoren Wang, **Huan Liao**, Ziniu Li, Yuancheng Wang, Li Wang, Dongya Jia, Yuanzhe Chen, Xiulin Li, Zhuo Chen, Zhizheng Wu<sup>†</sup>

[**[Project]**](https://speechjudge.github.io/) <strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>
[**[Paper]**](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.07931) <strong><span class='show_paper_citations' data='4FA6C0AAAAAJ:qjMakFHDy7sC'></span></strong>

**Huan Liao**<sup>★</sup>, Haonan Han<sup>★</sup>, Kai Yang, Tianjiao Du, Rui Yang, Zunnan Xu, Qinmei Xu, Jingquan Liu, Jiasheng Lu, Xiu Li<sup>†</sup>
